02 — The science

We don’t measure sleep.
We listen to it.

A Somnia device sits in the room and does almost nothing. It doesn’t glow. It doesn’t beep. It listens to the house the way you listen to a house at night — for the sounds that don’t belong. Then it adjusts the dark until your body gives in.

“Sleep isn’t a state. It’s a room you walk into. Most people never find the door.”

Dr. Elin Voss — Head of Sleep Research

04 — Questions

Answered,
mostly.

Is my data private?
Your audio is processed inside the room and never uploaded. Nothing leaves the house. Nothing needs to.
Does Somnia work if I sleep alone?
Yes. Somnia actually prefers it. The model is calibrated for one sleeper, one set of sounds, one breathing pattern.
Can I cancel at any time?
Yes — from your account page, in two clicks. No questions asked, and the room stays warm for thirty days.
What happens at 03:00?
Nothing. That’s the deepest part of the night for most people. Somnia checks that you’re still breathing, and goes back to listening.
Why is the house so quiet?
Because it’s listening. You can hear it if you stop scrolling for a moment.
